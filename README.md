# GOAL: Global Omnichannel & Affinity Agent Lab

**GOAL** is an agentic Streamlit application powered by the **Qloo Cultural Intelligence API**, built for the Qloo Hackathon. GOAL proves that grounding AI agents in real cultural affinity data (250M+ entities across music, film, dining, travel, and more) enables practical, high-value workflows that generic LLM-only applications cannot replicate.

---

## 🚀 Key Workflows Included

1. **Architectural Lighting & Aesthetic Mood Matcher**: Translates client music and cinema preferences into precise Kelvin ratings, fixture designs, and custom ambient lighting specifications for electricians and interior designers.
2. **Senior Adaptive Culinary & Dining Navigator**: Cross-references nostalgic food preferences with crucial health constraints (chewing comfort, medication interactions, low-sodium/low-heat delivery).
3. **Rare-Disease Journey Companion**: Matches patient emotional states and energy levels with restorative low-cognitive ambient media and supportive micro-communities.
4. **Qloo Taste Graph Explorer**: Interactive explorer visualizing real-time cross-domain cultural correlations.
